The holdout template requires combination-level disjointness. A held-out family
must vary at least one declared axis among coefficient structure, boundary,
region connection, topology, source/resource and scenario. It includes typed
pending family, candidate, scenario, input, provider, oracle and membership
receipt identities, with result `not_executed`.

The seven leakage controls cover candidate identity, topology/interface unit,
scenario trajectories, hidden oracle answers, partitioned solver cache,

remain unavailable until terminal/checker stages; independent replay requires a
fresh process/environment and frozen identity recipe. No held-out artifact or
combination is populated in the package.
