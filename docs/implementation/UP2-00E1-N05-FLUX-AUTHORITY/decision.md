# E1 N05 flux authority

This additive slice defines the candidate-owned typed N05 declaration boundary. It consumes a source-validated `CandidateStatePackageV5` and a source-validated E0 binding, then binds an authored flux/traction/current law to exact G1 occurrence and G2 interface context. G2 normal, E0 pending-port values, and G1 equation input units are never treated as flux authority.

Missing endpoint, law, sign, unit provenance, or cross-layer declaration remains `terminal_deferred`. A present typed candidate/ref/hash/axis/occurrence/interface/sign/unit contradiction is `fail_closed`; unexpected E0 or E1 checker faults remain `program_exception` and are not rewritten as a contract contradiction. The declaration contains an editable typed law expression/operator/relation whose content hash covers its endpoint signs and authored flux unit. Authored flux-law-unit provenance is a distinct N05 source locator; G1 occurrence provenance remains separate and its equation unit is never promoted to flux authority. The declaration hash is a downstream bound-input component and does not rewrite the original package or E0 hashes.

The five provisional UP0-02 rows remain historical. This implementation uses a versioned E1 fixture and receipt schema. It grants contract-only scope and no B00/B04, provider, mesh, engineering, physical, or R01-R10 credit.

Mutation dispatch is bound to the registered typed `field_path` and `mutation_kind` pair. The receipt `id` and fixture `expected_*` values are labels/oracles only; a mismatched path/kind is rejected, and `error_path` is emitted from the registered changed field. G1 occurrence paths/types, authored law paths/types, and endpoint/unit source paths/types are checked against their canonical joins.
